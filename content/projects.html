🎓 Pothole Detection - Research Thesis 2023
My research thesis focused on developing an advanced computer vision system for automatic pothole detection on road surfaces. The project leverages deep learning and object detection models to identify and classify road defects with high precision for infrastructure maintenance.
The system was designed using state-of-the-art deep learning frameworks and computer vision techniques. It processes road images to detect and localize potholes, providing valuable data for infrastructure management. The model achieves excellent detection accuracy across various road conditions and lighting scenarios.
